Opening a UK bank account after arriving

Prepare for the bank’s checks without assuming every provider needs the same documents. This checklist is for your own use; do not send identity documents or account details to RichQuid.

1. Choose the account

  • Read the specific account’s age, residence and eligibility requirements.
  • Confirm whether you can apply online, in an app or at a branch.
  • Check monthly fees, cash access, overseas payment charges and support.
  • Separate the payment account from any optional overdraft application.

2. Prepare the evidence the bank accepts

  • Check your identity document is valid and accepted.
  • Ask which address documents fit your situation, including alternatives if you have no utility bill.
  • Check document dates and whether the bank needs originals, PDFs or another format.
  • Keep your name and address consistent; explain differences honestly.
  • Have your address history and tax-residence information available.
  • If immigration evidence is requested, check the bank’s current eVisa or share-code instructions.

3. Apply and follow up

  • Use the bank’s official website, app or branch for documents.
  • Keep the application reference privately and note any requested next action.
  • If delayed, ask what information is missing before applying again.
  • Maintain a way to pay essential expenses while the application is considered.
