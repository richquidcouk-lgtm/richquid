## RichQuid's backlink growth strategy — 5 plays

### Play 1: Make the calculators link-magnets

Calculators are one of the most linkable UK finance content types — people cite them in articles ("use this stamp duty calculator…"). What to do:

- Add an **"Embed this calculator"** snippet on each tool page (provides an iframe + script with attribution back to RichQuid).
- Submit each calculator to **Product Hunt** (calculators do well there).
- Submit to **directory pages** like SaaSHub, AlternativeTo, Pinkbike-style finance tool directories.
- Pitch the most useful (stamp duty, mortgage affordability, take-home pay) to UK property/mortgage blogs as "ready-made tools your readers can use."

**Why this works**: every embed = a dofollow backlink. Tools embed more than articles do.

### Play 2: HARO / source-of-sources / journalist outreach

Far higher ROI than guest posting for finance content:

- **Connectively (formerly HARO)** — free journalist query service. Sign up; UK money journalists post requests for expert quotes daily.
- **Source of Sources** — same model.
- **#JournoRequest** on Twitter/X — UK journalists post requests.
- **Qwoted** — pitch platform.

For each: pitch RichQuid's Clara Penny (or whoever the editorial face becomes — resolve this first per the SEO audit) as a finance commentator. When you get quoted in a UK national outlet, you typically get a backlink. **One BBC Money citation = years of guest-post equivalent value.**

Time investment: 30 minutes per day pitching = realistic 1–2 quality citations per month.

### Play 3: Industry trade press citations

## What NOT to do

A few things commonly recommended that don&rsquo;t work in UK finance:

- **Buying "DR 50+ finance backlinks" packages on Fiverr/Upwork** — these are almost universally low-quality PBN links that risk Google penalty. Don't.
- **Mass guest-posting to low-DR finance blogs** — wastes time, weakens your link profile.
- **Generic blog comments** — no longer effective and looks spammy.
- **Reciprocal links** ("you link to me, I link to you") — also flagged.
