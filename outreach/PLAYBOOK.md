# RichQuid Outreach Playbook

**Built**: 2026-05-23
**Goal**: Build authoritative backlinks to richquid.co.uk via free, sustainable, daily activity.
**Cadence**: Daily 30–60 minute commitment. Target 5–10 placements/month in month 1, ramping to 30+/month by month 6.

---

## The honest reality

Most "daily guest posting" advice online is unrealistic. Editor pools at quality UK finance sites are small and slow — you'll get 1–3 acceptances per week at best, even doing everything right. Many "finance guest posting" lists are link farms that will *hurt* SEO, not help.

This playbook commits to a **daily action**, not a daily publication. The action mixes:

1. **Guest posts** to vetted UK finance/property/business sites (slow, high-quality)
2. **Journalist citations** via Connectively/HARO/SoS — UK money journalists asking for expert quotes
3. **Owned platforms** (Medium, LinkedIn, HubPages, Substack) — instant publishing for authority signals
4. **Forum engagement** (MoneySavingExpert forums, Reddit r/UKPersonalFinance, Mumsnet) — slow-burn link-building from quality forums
5. **Calculator embed outreach** — cold pitch UK mortgage brokers, accountants, finance bloggers to embed RichQuid calculators

Done well, this produces compounding authority over 6–12 months. Done poorly, it wastes time on link farms or generates AI-spam guest posts that hurt the brand.

---

## Quality bar — NEVER violate

- **DR ≥ 25** on target site (use Ahrefs free DR checker at https://ahrefs.com/website-authority-checker)
- **No PBNs** — if the site has obvious link-farm signals (unrelated topics, no editorial standards, identical templates to 5 other sites, only outbound link sales), skip it
- **No paid placements** — guest post fees, sponsored content fees, etc. are out of scope
- **Real editorial standards** preferred — sites that occasionally reject pitches are higher-value than sites that publish anything
- **Original content** for every guest post — never re-use a RichQuid article verbatim; the angle and structure should be host-site-specific
- **No "vs RichQuid" framing in guest posts** — compete on quality of standalone content, not by namedropping ourselves vs competitors
- **Cite HMRC/gov.uk/MoneyHelper** for any statutory claim
- **Educational tone only** — no personalised advice, no named-provider rate claims, no guarantees

## The weekly rotation

A repeatable daily commitment of ~30–60 minutes. Aim for 5 of 7 days actively engaged; the system tolerates slips.

| Day | Activity | Time | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | **Connectively / HARO pitches** | 30 min | 3 journalist queries answered |
| Tuesday | **Draft guest post** for next target | 45 min | One ~1,000-word draft ready |
| Wednesday | **Refresh target list** | 20 min | 2 new vetted targets added; expired ones removed |
| Thursday | **Submit Tuesday's draft** + follow up Monday pitches | 30 min | 1 submission sent; 1–2 follow-ups |
| Friday | **Owned platform piece** | 45 min | Publish on Medium / LinkedIn / HubPages (rotate) |
| Saturday | **Calculator embed outreach** | 30 min | 5 cold-emails sent to brokers/accountants |
| Sunday | **Forum engagement + plan next week** | 20 min | 3 quality answers on MSE forum/Reddit/Mumsnet |

If a day is missed: prioritise Connectively (highest ROI/minute) over everything else. Owned-platform publishing is the easiest catch-up activity.

## Channel 2: Journalist citations (highest ROI/minute)

**Connectively** (formerly HARO) — free journalist query service. UK money journalists post requests for expert quotes daily.

**Process**:

1. Sign up at https://www.connectively.us
2. Filter for "Finance", "Business", "UK"
3. Twice a day, 10 minutes: scan for relevant queries
4. Pitch only when you genuinely have expertise on the topic
5. Keep pitches under 200 words: who you are, your angle, 2-3 quotable lines, link to RichQuid

**Pitch template** (adapt per query):

> Hi [journalist name],
>
> Re: [your query about X]
>
> I'm the editorial lead at RichQuid (richquid.co.uk), a UK personal finance site focused on tax-current explainers and calculators showing the maths. Happy to provide an expert quote.
>
> [2-3 sentences of substantive, quotable content directly answering the query.]
>
> Background: [one line on credentials/site/coverage].
>
> Happy to expand or provide additional angles. Available [today/this week] for any follow-up.
>
> Best,
> Clara Penny
> Editorial, RichQuid
> richquid.co.uk

**Alternatives**:

- **Source of Sources** (sourceofsources.com) — same model, free
- **Qwoted** (qwoted.com) — same model, free tier available
- **#JournoRequest** on Twitter/X — UK journalists tag queries

A single BBC/Telegraph/Guardian citation = years of guest-post equivalent value. Connectively is the highest-leverage activity in this entire playbook.

## Channel 4: Forum engagement (slow burn)

The unsexy, slow-compounding channel. Don't link-drop. Answer questions substantively; readers click through to RichQuid because the answer was helpful.

**Communities** (Sunday 20 minutes):

- **MoneySavingExpert forums** — answer 1-2 questions on ISAs, pensions, tax. Link to a RichQuid guide only when it's the directly relevant best answer.
- **Reddit r/UKPersonalFinance** — same approach. Quality answers earn upvotes and trust.
- **Mumsnet "Money matters"** — UK parent/mum finance discussions. Different audience to MSE — older, often higher-income.
- **Reddit r/UKInvesting**, **r/HENRYUK**, **r/UKJobs** (for side-hustle tax) — narrower communities

**Rules**:

- Never start a thread to plug a RichQuid link
- Answer 3-5 questions substantively before posting your first RichQuid link
- Only link when it's *the most helpful possible* answer
- Disclose: "I write for RichQuid, our take here is [link]"

This is the only channel that won't show measurable backlink growth quickly, but it builds *brand* in a way Google increasingly cares about.

## Channel 5: Calculator embed outreach (Saturday)

We just shipped the embed system. Now leverage it.

**Process** (5 cold emails per Saturday):

1. Identify a UK mortgage broker / accountancy firm / personal finance blogger
2. Find their blog or "tools" page
3. Send a short email offering the relevant calculator embed

**Pitch template**:

> Subject: Free [calculator name] for your readers
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I came across your post on [specific recent article]. The calculation in there is exactly what our [stamp duty / take-home pay / mortgage affordability] calculator covers — except ours lets readers plug in their own numbers and see every line of working.
>
> If it'd be useful, you're welcome to embed it free on your site:
>
> https://www.richquid.co.uk/tools/[slug]
>
> One-click "embed" iframe code is on the calculator page. We just ask the "Powered by RichQuid" link stays visible — nothing else.
>
> Best,
> Clara Penny
> RichQuid
> https://www.richquid.co.uk

**Target sources to find brokers/accountants**:

- UK mortgage broker directories
- ICAEW practice finder
- Personal finance UK blogger lists (FeedSpot UK Personal Finance)
- Twitter/LinkedIn search: "mortgage broker UK" + "blog"

Conversion rate is low (~3-5% will respond positively). 5 emails/week × 50 weeks × 4% = ~10 embeds/year. Each embed is a dofollow backlink with high topical relevance.

## Red flags — sites to skip

If you encounter any of these on a "guest posting" target, walk away:

- Asks for payment to publish (we're free-only)
- Topics span unrelated niches (one site covering "finance, gambling, escort services, crypto, casino" is a PBN)
- Identical template/footer to multiple other sites
- DR <20 by Ahrefs DR checker
- Recent guest posts are obviously AI-generated or low-quality
- Site explicitly resells/sells guest post placements as a business
- "Drop your link here" forums or wikis
- Sites that publish anything submitted within hours (no editorial)
- Footer links to obvious link farms

## Monthly review

Last day of each month, review the log:

- Total placements this month
- Channel mix

Adjust the rotation if a channel isn't producing — but give each at least 6 weeks before pulling the plug.
