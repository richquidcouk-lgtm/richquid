## How to use

- Add a row when you pitch, draft, submit, or hear back.
- Update Status as it progresses: `Drafted` → `Pitched` → `Accepted` / `Declined` → `Published`.
- Fill URL + DR after a placement goes live.
- Weekly review every Sunday — count placements, look at channel mix, decide what to adjust.
