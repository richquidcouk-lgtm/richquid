To open a UK current account after moving here, check the **specific account's eligibility rules**, then prepare the identity, address and immigration evidence that provider requests. There is no universal document checklist or guaranteed approval time across all banks. A passport and UK address are useful starting points, but neither guarantees acceptance.

## What should I prepare before applying?

Keep your name and address consistent across your application and documents. Have an in-date identity document, contact details, address history and information about your tax residence ready. The bank may request more information or check records electronically. Do not send identity documents to RichQuid; upload them only through the bank's official channel.

For immigration status, use the current eVisa process where requested. All biometric residence permits have expired; an old BRP should not be described as current proof of immigration status. See [GOV.UK's BRP and eVisa guidance](https://www.gov.uk/biometric-residence-permits). A share code proves the information it was issued for; ask the bank which evidence and code it accepts.

## How do provider requirements differ?

| Provider example | What to check before applying | Official starting point |
|---|---|---|
| HSBC UK | Account eligibility, identity/address evidence and whether immigration evidence is needed; upload requirements differ from postal certification | [Bank account eligibility](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/current-accounts/products/bank-account/) |
| Monzo | UK residence, right to reside and the identity/address documents requested for your application | [Personal account opening](https://monzo.com/help/opening-an-account/how-to-open-a-Monzo-Personal-Account) |

These are examples of published application routes, not a ranking, an approval guarantee or a claim that other providers will decline you. Check current product terms directly. Avoid choosing an account solely because someone reports being approved quickly.

## What if I do not have a utility bill yet?

Ask the bank which alternative documents it accepts for your circumstances. A university letter, government correspondence or another named document may be suitable for a particular provider. Do not assume an employer letter, tenancy agreement or mobile bill is accepted everywhere.

For example, HSBC's [identification guide](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/help/banking-made-easy/help-us-identify-you/) describes several address documents and online upload requirements. Use the list for the application channel you are using, including the document's age and format. A PDF statement may be acceptable where a screenshot is not.

For a broader checklist, read [documents for a UK bank account](/guides/what-documents-do-i-need-to-open-a-uk-bank-account).

## Does having no UK credit history stop me opening an account?

A current account and an overdraft are different products. Borrowing features involve separate eligibility checks. Check whether an account without an overdraft or an appropriate basic bank account is available if you do not qualify for the original product. Do not assume that approval for a bank account guarantees a credit card. See [credit cards with no UK history](/guides/what-credit-cards-can-i-get-with-no-uk-credit-history).

## What if my application is delayed or refused?

Check for requests in the bank's official app or messages, confirm your submitted details, and ask whether more evidence is needed. Do not repeatedly submit conflicting applications or alter information to fit a product. A provider may not be able to explain every decision. If you believe the process was mishandled, use its formal complaints route.

Keep another way to meet essential expenses while the application is being considered. We do not promise same-day opening, a fixed processing window or a particular credit limit.

## How should I compare an account once I am eligible?

Check monthly fees, everyday payment features, access to cash, overseas transfers, foreign-currency charges and customer support. Separate bank deposits from e-money: the protections can differ, so read the provider's current legal entity and protection information. For eligible bank deposits, the standard [FSCS limit](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/banks-building-societies-credit-unions/deposit-limit/) is £120,000 per person per authorised firm, with shared-licence and eligibility rules.

Once the account is working, build a practical budget with our [take-home pay calculator](/tools/take-home-pay-calculator) and [emergency fund calculator](/tools/emergency-fund-calculator). Only choose a rewards account after checking whether its fees and conditions suit the payments you actually make.

## A practical document comparison for new arrivals

These examples were checked on 18 September 2026. They are not an exhaustive list or an approval ranking.

| Application route | Published evidence examples | A useful question before applying |
|---|---|---|
| HSBC | Its identification guidance lists identity and address documents, including account-specific student evidence and eVisa checks | Which section applies to my account and online or branch application? |
| Monzo | Its personal-account guidance lists valid photo ID and, if address evidence is requested, options including an active UK tenancy agreement or specified recent official correspondence | Is my document current, in an accepted format and suitable for this particular check? |

Use [HSBC's identification guidance](https://www.hsbc.co.uk/help/banking-made-easy/help-us-identify-you/) and [Monzo's personal-account instructions](https://monzo.com/help/opening-an-account/how-to-open-a-Monzo-Personal-Account). Neither list means every applicant with that document will be accepted. Monzo also specifies UK residence/right to reside and a compatible mobile device. A document suitable for one bank is not automatically suitable for another.

## Your application preparation checklist

- Choose a specific account and check its age, residence and application-channel requirements.
- Confirm which identity document you will use and whether it is in date.
- Ask about address alternatives before ordering or paying for new paperwork.
- Have accurate address history, tax-residence details and an explanation of the account's intended use ready.
- Check fees and whether an overdraft is optional or requires a separate application.
- Use only the provider's official app, website or branch for sensitive documents.
- Keep a note of the application reference and the bank's next requested action.

## Example: arriving before the first utility bill

Imagine you have a passport and an active tenancy but no utility bill in your name. Do not assume you must wait months, and do not assume every bank accepts the tenancy. Compare the provider lists above, ask about your available documents and use the route it confirms. If additional evidence is requested, ask what would resolve the specific missing check before submitting a second application elsewhere.
