**Ask two separate questions: are you eligible to subscribe to an ISA, and will your chosen provider accept this payment route?** Having money overseas does not answer either question. Do not send an international payment to an ISA until the provider confirms the account details, currency and sender requirements.

## Does the country of my bank account decide ISA eligibility?

No. The [government's ISA eligibility rules](https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts) concern matters including age and UK residence, with specific exceptions for certain Crown employees and their partners. A UK bank account or British passport is not, by itself, proof that you can subscribe.

If you become non-UK resident, you can generally keep an existing ISA but cannot make new subscriptions unless an exception applies. Tell the provider about your move. [GOV.UK explains the non-resident position](https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts/if-you-move-abroad).

## Can I transfer directly from an overseas account?

That depends on the provider's payment instructions. Ask it to confirm these points in writing:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Must the payment originate from a nominated UK account in my name? | A sender or account mismatch can require further checks or lead to a returned payment. |
| Are international transfers accepted for this particular ISA? | Instructions for a general investment account may not apply to its ISA. |
| Which currencies and payment methods are supported? | Do not assume the ISA will perform currency conversion. |
| Are payments from remittance services accepted? | The sending account information may differ from a bank transfer made directly by you. |
| What evidence of the money's source is required? | Keep the statements and transfer trail before sending funds. |
| Is there a funding deadline? | Allow time for checks and returned payments rather than relying on an instant transfer. |

For example, [Hargreaves Lansdown's Cash ISA information](https://www.hl.co.uk/savings/cash-isa/how-it-works) links to the terms for that product. Use the actual product's instructions, not a rule copied from a different account. This guide does not certify a list of providers accepting overseas transfers.

## What if the provider requires a UK bank account first?

If you are eligible and the provider confirms that route, you can arrange the currency conversion into a suitable account you hold, then make the ISA subscription using its accepted payment method. Compare the total pounds received after fees and the exchange-rate spread. Keep both transfers' records.

The [new-arrival bank account checklist](/guides/how-to-open-a-bank-account-as-a-new-arrival-to-the-uk) can help you prepare documents. Opening an account does not settle tax residence or the treatment of overseas income.

## Worked example: overseas savings and a partly used allowance

Assume you are eligible to subscribe in 2026/27, have already paid £5,000 into adult ISAs, and convert overseas savings into £12,000 in your UK current account. You then pay £10,000 of that into your ISA through the accepted route.

- The bank transfer into the current account is not itself an ISA subscription.
- Your new subscription is £10,000.
- Total subscriptions are £5,000 + £10,000 = £15,000.
- Against the £20,000 annual limit, £5,000 remains, assuming no other subscriptions or adjustments.

Use the [ISA allowance tracker](/tools/isa-allowance-tracker) to add subscriptions across accounts. The calculation does not determine your eligibility or tax on the money before it enters the ISA.

## Is this the same as transferring an existing ISA?

No. Moving existing ISA money between providers uses the [official ISA transfer process](/guides/transferring-isa-uk). Moving money out yourself and paying it back can have different consequences. Existing overseas savings outside an ISA do not become an ISA transfer just because the payment crosses a border.

## What should I confirm before paying?

Check residence and subscription eligibility, unused allowance, accepted payment route, sender details, conversion costs and any special rules for the ISA type. If the money includes overseas income or gains, its tax treatment needs a separate review; putting it into an ISA does not retrospectively settle that issue.

Source and payment-route guidance reviewed 18 September 2026. Provider acceptance must be checked for your own account.
