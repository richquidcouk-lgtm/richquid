A reward current account can pay a fixed reward or cashback on specified transactions. **Subtract the account fee and check the monthly conditions** before comparing it with a fee-free account. A one-off switching bonus is separate from the recurring value.

## Which rewards should I compare?

| Reward type | Calculation | Main checks |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed monthly reward | Qualifying months × reward, less fees | Required deposits, direct debits, login or other conditions |
| Cashback on bills | Eligible bills × rates, subject to caps | Which billers and payment methods qualify |
| Debit-card cashback | Eligible card spending × rate, subject to caps | Merchant exclusions, promotion length and spending limits |

The product's current terms decide whether a payment qualifies. Not every utility payment, transfer or subscription counts. [MoneyHelper describes different cashback routes](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/blog/everyday-money/four-ways-to-get-cashback).

## What is the net annual value?

Use ordinary spending, not spending you would add just to earn a reward. These are hypothetical calculations, not current bank offers:

- Account A pays £5 in each qualifying month and costs £3 monthly. If you qualify every month, net annual value is **12 × (£5 − £3) = £24**.
- If you qualify in only eight months but pay the fee for all twelve, value falls to **8 × £5 − 12 × £3 = £4**.
- Account B pays 1% on £600 monthly eligible bills but caps rewards at £5 monthly and costs £2 monthly. Net annual value is **12 × (£5 − £2) = £36**.

The rate alone does not identify the better account. Account B's gross reward is £5, not £6, because the cap applies first. Add overdraft interest or other relevant charges before deciding whether the reward makes the account worthwhile.

## How should I treat a switching bonus?

Check the deadline, previous-customer exclusions and the exact switching requirements. Do not assume a bonus will repeat annually. Compare first-year value separately from later years, and make sure your bills, income and essential banking services continue to work during the switch.

## Does meeting a deposit requirement make me better off?

It only helps if you satisfy the terms without creating another cost. Check whether internal transfers qualify and whether the account requires a minimum balance rather than a monthly inflow. Do not use an overdraft or miss another payment merely to chase a small reward.

## Current-account rewards or a cashback credit card?

They can apply to different payment types. A current-account direct debit and a card purchase are not interchangeable. A card also involves borrowing, eligibility and repayment conditions. Our [cashback credit-card guide](/guides/uk-cashback-credit-cards-explained) compares fees, spending tiers and caps using worked examples.

## What should my comparison sheet contain?

Write down the monthly fee, reward calculation, cap, eligible payments, qualification conditions, standard rate after promotion, overdraft terms and where you checked each fact. Check the provider's product summary and full terms immediately before applying; we do not maintain a live league table of bank offers.

Separate deposit protection from rewards. Eligible bank deposits are normally protected up to **£120,000 per person per authorised firm** under the current [FSCS deposit rules](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/banks-building-societies-credit-unions/deposit-limit/). Two brands can share a licence. Other account types may have different protections.
