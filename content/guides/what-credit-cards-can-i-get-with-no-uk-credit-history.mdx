A limited UK credit history can make borrowing harder, but **no credit history is not the same as bad credit**. A credit-builder card or a lender's existing-customer route may be worth checking if you meet its criteria. Neither guarantees approval. Use an eligibility checker that explicitly says it uses a soft search before deciding whether to make a full application.

## Which routes can I investigate?

| Route | What to check | What it does not promise |
|---|---|---|
| Credit-builder card | Minimum residence/address history, income, affordability, APR and eligibility-check terms | Approval without checks or a particular limit |
| Existing bank's card | Whether there is an offer or eligibility checker in your official banking app | Your current account does not automatically qualify you for borrowing |
| Existing international card relationship | Whether your issuer has a published relocation application route | Automatic transfer of an overseas credit score, debt or limit |

We do not publish an unverified list of cards supposedly accepting every new arrival. Product availability, residency rules and pricing change. Read the current issuer page rather than relying on a historic APR or an online anecdote.

## What is a credit-builder card?

It is a borrowing product aimed at people building or rebuilding a credit record. Interest can be expensive, so compare the actual offer and repayment terms. Paying the full statement balance by its due date normally avoids purchase interest where the product's interest-free conditions are met; cash withdrawals and other transaction types can work differently. [MoneyHelper's credit-card guide](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/everyday-money/credit/simple-guide-to-credit-cards) explains the main types and costs.

**Worked example:** suppose you use a card for £40 of planned spending each month and pay the £40 statement in full on time. You are not required to carry an interest-bearing balance to show repayment activity. The purpose is a manageable record of payments, not spending more to reach a target score.

## Can I use my overseas American Express relationship?

American Express publishes a [UK moving-abroad application route](https://www.americanexpress.com/en-gb/company/about/moving-abroad/) for eligible existing cardmembers. Check its current membership and residence conditions and provide the requested details. This is an application for a new local card, not an assurance of approval or a transfer of an existing balance. It is not evidence that every UK Amex applicant can import foreign credit data through a particular third-party service.

## Does no UK credit history mean no credit check?

No. A lender can still assess identity, affordability and available credit records. An eligibility result is an indication, not an offer. Check whether a full application will leave a hard search, and avoid repeated applications simply because one lender declines you.

A secured-card description from another country is not proof a comparable UK product is available. Do not pay a deposit or application fee to an unfamiliar service on the promise of guaranteed approval.

## How can I build a useful credit record?

Keep personal details consistent, pay existing commitments on time and check the information held about you. Register to vote if you are eligible under the rules where you live. Correct mistakes through the relevant credit-reference agency or provider rather than paying a service promising to erase accurate information. [MoneyHelper explains practical credit-record checks](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/everyday-money/credit/how-to-improve-your-credit-score).

If you need an everyday payment account first, follow the [new-arrival banking guide](/guides/how-to-open-a-bank-account-as-a-new-arrival-to-the-uk). You do not need to borrow just to receive wages or manage a budget.

## What should I do after a refusal?

Pause before making another application. Check the published eligibility criteria, your address details and credit records. Ask the lender whether it can explain the decision or what its complaints route is if you believe an error occurred. There is no fixed number of months after arrival that guarantees acceptance.

## Should I choose rewards while building credit?

Affordable repayments and suitable eligibility come first. A small cashback percentage cannot make expensive borrowing cheap. Our [cashback card guide](/guides/uk-cashback-credit-cards-explained) shows how fees and caps affect rewards; use it only after deciding that a credit card is suitable for your circumstances.
