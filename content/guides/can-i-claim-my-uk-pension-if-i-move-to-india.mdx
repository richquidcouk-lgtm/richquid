**You can receive a UK pension while living in India, subject to your entitlement and scheme arrangements. Moving your pension payments is different from transferring the pension itself.** State Pension increases, private pension access and tax treatment need separate checks.

## Will my UK State Pension increase while I live in India?

India is not currently a country where the UK State Pension receives annual increases. Starting your claim before you leave the UK does not preserve annual increases once you live in India. Give the pension authorities accurate information about where you live. [GOV.UK lists the countries and explains the uprating rules](https://www.gov.uk/state-pension-if-you-retire-abroad/rates-of-state-pension).

**Correction, 18 September 2026:** an earlier version suggested that claiming before departure and retaining a UK address could preserve increases. That was incorrect and has been removed.

Do not calculate your entitlement from a generic weekly headline rate. Obtain your own [State Pension forecast](https://www.gov.uk/check-state-pension) and confirm the position with the [International Pension Centre](https://www.gov.uk/international-pension-centre), especially if you have pre-2016 or overseas contribution history.

## What changes for different pension types?

| Pension or action | What to check before moving |
|---|---|
| UK State Pension | Your entitlement, claim arrangements, residence notification and whether annual increases apply. |
| Workplace or personal pension left in the UK | Scheme access rules, supported payment destinations, charges and tax handling. |
| Government-service pension | Whether a separate treaty article applies to your pension and circumstances. |
| Transfer into an overseas pension scheme | Transfer eligibility, advice requirements, tax charges and the protections you would lose or gain. |

Keeping a UK pension does not necessarily mean keeping the same bank payment arrangements. Ask each provider; do not assume all schemes can pay every overseas account or currency.

## How does the UK–India tax treaty affect a pension?

The [UK–India treaty published by HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/india-tax-treaties) distinguishes ordinary pensions and annuities from government-service pensions. Article 20 generally assigns qualifying ordinary pensions to the residence state; Article 19 contains separate government-service rules. Residence, pension type and the nature of a payment matter.

Do not assume the UK treatment of a lump sum is reproduced in India, or that a provider will automatically stop deducting UK tax. Ask HMRC and an adviser familiar with both countries about the correct relief procedure and Indian reporting. This guide does not calculate Indian income tax or establish your treaty residence.

## Do I need to transfer the pension to India?

Receiving payments abroad does not require transferring the pension pot abroad. These are separate decisions. [GOV.UK's overseas transfer guidance](https://www.gov.uk/transferring-your-pension/transferring-to-an-overseas-pension-scheme) explains QROPS requirements, the possible 25% overseas transfer charge, available allowance and residence conditions. A scheme's inclusion on a list is not a personal recommendation.

Compare retaining the UK scheme with transferring only after understanding fees, investment options, withdrawal rules, currency exposure, tax and protection. There is no pension-pot size at which a transfer becomes automatically worthwhile.

## A practical pre-move checklist

1. List each pension separately: provider, scheme type, reference and contact channel.
2. Obtain a current forecast or benefit statement rather than assuming your entitlement.
3. Tell the relevant authorities and providers your new residence and contact details.
4. Ask how payments, exchange rates and bank charges work, and how any requested life certificate should be returned.
5. Confirm tax residence, pension classification and relief paperwork before making a large withdrawal.
6. Keep a record of correspondence and payment instructions in a secure place.

For claiming arrangements, start with the [official State Pension abroad service](https://www.gov.uk/state-pension-if-you-retire-abroad). Do not rely on old phone numbers or unverified promises of a particular processing time.

## Worked example: compare the payment route, not just the headline pension

Suppose a private scheme can pay £1,000 monthly before any tax or charges. Route A has a £5 transfer charge; route B has no stated transfer fee but a less favourable exchange rate. Compare the rupees actually received for the same £1,000 on the same date. A zero advertised fee does not establish the cheaper route.

Repeat the comparison for the scheme's actual payment frequency. Keep the decision about transferring the pension pot separate from this payment-cost exercise.
